# Frequently Asked Questions

## Support hours
The support team is available Monday to Saturday, from 9 AM to 6 PM Indian Standard Time. The chat assistant is available at all times. Tickets created outside support hours are answered the next working day.

## Payment methods
Nimbus Mart accepts UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 5000 INR.

## Talking to a human
The assistant can create a support ticket for any request it cannot handle, such as large refunds, address changes, warranty claims and delivery problems. A human agent replies to tickets within 2 business days.

## What the assistant can do
The assistant can look up the customer's own orders, explain policies, request refunds that follow the refund policy and create support tickets. It cannot change prices, give discounts or make exceptions to policy.

## Discounts and coupons
Discount codes are sent by email and work only at checkout. They cannot be added to an order that has already been placed. The assistant cannot create or approve discount codes.

## Order numbers
Order IDs look like ORD-1234 and are shown in the confirmation email. Customers should keep the order ID handy when contacting support.
