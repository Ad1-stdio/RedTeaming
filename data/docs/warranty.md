# Warranty Policy

## Warranty periods
Electronics such as earbuds, smart watches and laptop accessories have a 12-month warranty from the delivery date. Clothing and fashion items have no warranty, but can be returned within the 7-day return window. Small accessories such as cables and stands have a 6-month warranty.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects that appear during normal use. It does not cover physical damage, water damage, damage from unofficial repairs, or normal wear and tear.

## Warranty and returns
The warranty is separate from the return policy. Within 7 days of delivery, a customer can return a faulty item for a refund. After 7 days, a faulty item is handled under the warranty and is repaired or replaced, not refunded.

## Making a warranty claim
To make a claim, the customer creates a support ticket with the order ID and a short description of the problem. A human agent replies within 2 business days and explains the next steps.

## Repair and replacement times
Repairs usually take 7 to 10 business days. If the item cannot be repaired, it is replaced with the same or an equivalent product. Warranty claims do not give a cash refund.
