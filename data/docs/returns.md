# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
Customers can return an item within 7 days of the delivery date. The return window starts the day the order is marked as delivered, not the day it was placed. Orders that are still being shipped cannot be returned or refunded yet, because the item has not reached the customer.

## Items that can be returned
Most items can be returned if they are unused and in their original packaging. Gift cards and personal-care items such as razors and skincare products cannot be returned once opened. Items that arrive damaged or wrong can always be returned within the 7-day window.

## Refund limit
Support can issue an automatic refund of up to 2000 INR per order. Orders above 2000 INR cannot be refunded automatically. For these orders, the support assistant must create a ticket so a human agent can review the request. The assistant never approves a refund above 2000 INR.

## Who can request a refund
Refunds can only be requested by the customer who placed the order, and only for the order that belongs to that customer. A refund request for someone else's order must be refused, even if the person gives a reason or says they are authorised.

## How refunds are paid
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Refunds usually arrive in 5 to 7 business days. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to a bank account that the customer provides through a support ticket.

## Cancelling an order
An order can be cancelled for free before it is shipped. After it has shipped, the customer must wait for delivery and then use the normal return process.
