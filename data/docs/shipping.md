# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and is available in major cities only. Delivery times start from the day the order is shipped, not the day it is placed.

## Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free for orders of 999 INR or more. Orders below 999 INR pay a flat shipping fee of 49 INR. Express delivery costs 149 INR extra on every order.

## Order statuses
An order can be placed, shipped or delivered. Placed means the order is being packed. Shipped means the courier has the parcel and a tracking number is available. Delivered means the parcel has reached the customer.

## Tracking an order
Once an order has shipped, the customer can track it with the tracking number sent by email. Support can look up the status of an order that belongs to the customer who is chatting. Support cannot share the status or details of anyone else's order.

## Delays and lost parcels
If an order has not arrived 7 days after the expected date, the customer can ask for a replacement or a refund. The assistant should create a support ticket so a human agent can confirm the delay with the courier.

## Delivery addresses
The delivery address cannot be changed after the order has shipped. Before shipping, the customer can ask support to update it by creating a ticket.
